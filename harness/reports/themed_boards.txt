themed boards: is the clue number collapsing because the board is dense?
=======================================================================
python -m harness.themed_probe          (20 seeds/pool, offline, mocked LLM)

The organisers ran us on a board of 25 gaming / internet-culture words and saw
18 consecutive number-1 clues and a 19-turn solo win, while the baseline GPT
team cleared it with INTERNET 4 / COMPUTER 3 / SPACE 2.

  top2-own      over the similarity table's clue vocabulary, how often the two
                board words a clue pulls hardest on are BOTH ours.  Mock-free.
  own-in-top3   mean own words among a clue's three strongest.  Mock-free.
  clues         how many clue/board pairs were scorable at all.
  mock numbers  a full get_clue with brainstorm and panel both stood in for by
                the table (brainstorm sees only our words, panel ranks all 25).
  0.29.0        every LLM call returning nothing, which is what their SDK did.

Verdict: the streak is not a property of the board.

* With the brainstorm dead the number is 1 on EVERY pool, the default framework
  pool included -- 100% of clues, all eight rows.  The no-panel branch takes
  heuristic candidates, which claim no targets, so its number is
  min(2, len(targets) or 1, ...) = 1 by construction.  That is the whole
  18-clue streak, and it is the 0.29.0 regression, not the theme.
* Give the same board a working panel and the number does not collapse: the
  organisers' own 25 words come out at mean 2.00 with not one number-1 clue,
  against 2.05 on the default pool.
* The density statistic agrees.  Their board is 12.0% top2-own against the
  default pool's 11.2% -- marginally denser, nowhere near a collapse.

So no pacing change was made.  Loosening the number rule here would spend the
risk budget the parked `ambitious` preset already failed to earn, against a
symptom that has a different cause and is now fixed.

SECOND RUN, 2026-08-03, after the board vocabulary was widened
--------------------------------------------------------------
The first run carried a caveat: the shipped table's board vocabulary was the
framework pool plus the slang pool (1008 words), and themed-kitchen and
themed-sport were almost entirely outside it -- 0 and 114 scorable clue/board
pairs, so those rows measured table coverage and not board density.  The table
now covers every themed pool outright, so every row below is a density
measurement, and the earlier themed rows should be discarded rather than
compared against.

What that changes: nothing in the verdict, and it removes the last place the
verdict was resting on missing data.  The themed pools measure 10.0%-13.0%
top2-own against the default pool's 11.2%, and their mock numbers are 1.90-2.15
against 2.05 -- the same band, not a collapse.  The old themed rows that looked
like a collapse (mean 1.10 on themed-sport, 90% of clues at 1) were the mock
brainstorm having almost no board words it could price, which is the sensor
being blind, not the board being dense.

The default and slang rows moved slightly too (10.7% -> 11.2%, 32055 -> 32240
scorable pairs) because the rebuild added 105 clue words and re-fitted the
codebook.  Both are inside the noise of a 20-seed sample.
