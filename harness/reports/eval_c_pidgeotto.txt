NOTE ON PROVENANCE
Measured on the WITHDRAWN aggressive clue-number variant, not on the config
now on main.  The headline result -- 85% red win rate, up from Pidgey's 70% --
is the validation of the two-team urgency fix (the guesser now takes the bonus
guess when the opponent is one or two words from winning, instead of demanding
leftover support it rarely has).  That fix IS on main.  The clue-number knobs
that were withdrawn also contributed here (red clue number averaged 1.85), so
this number is an upper bound on the shipped config, not a measurement of it.
